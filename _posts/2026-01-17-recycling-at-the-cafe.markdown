We're pleased to announce that we will now take--if we can't fix them--small electrical items, batteries, and bulbs for recycling at the Tregony Repair Café.
{:.lead}

Sometimes, despite our best efforts, an item simply can't be repaired. Rather than seeing it end up in landfill, we can now make sure it's recycled responsibly.

So if you bring something along and we're unable to fix it, don't worry—we'll take it off your hands and ensure it goes to the right place.

This is just another small way we're trying to reduce waste and look after our environment here in Tregony. See you at the next café!
