Our first repair café of the year is on **Saturday, February 28th, 1-3pm** at Tregony Village Hall. We then continue on the fourth Saturday of most months throughout the year.

Check the [full schedule](/cafeTimes/) for all dates, and follow us on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61553203681698) for any updates.
